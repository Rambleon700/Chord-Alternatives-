Interactive Ear Training Workouts

Try switching between these specific chord setups to train your ears to feel subtle pitch changes:

  • Major vs. Minor: Keep C as root. Alternate between C Major and C Minor to hear the single 1-semitone shift on the 3rd.
  • The Suspended Resolution: Select C Sus4, strum it, then quickly switch to C Major to feel the harmonic tension release.
  • Lush Major 7ths: Compare C Major to C Major 7th to hear the smooth, dreamy warmth added by the natural 7th degree.
